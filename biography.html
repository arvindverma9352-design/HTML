Dr. B.R. Ambedker biography

Dr. B.R. Ambedker, born on April 14, 1891, in Mhow, Madhya Pradesh, was a prominent Indian social reformer and the chief architect of the Indian Constitution. He faced severe caste discrimination but overcame it through education and determination. Ambedker played a pivotal   role in drafting the Indian Constitution, ensuring equality and justice for all citizens. He also fought against untouchability and
promoted the rights of Dalits and marginalized communities. His life and work continue to inspire movements for social justice in India.
